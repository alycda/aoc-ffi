Day 1: Historian Hysteria
===

Given two lists of numbers:
* Parse input into two separate lists
* Sort both lists
* Calculate sum of absolute differences

```rust
Input:
3   4
4   3
2   5

Output: Sum of |3-3| + |4-4| + |2-5| = 3
```
